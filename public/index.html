One path from enquiry to commission

Each step hands the next one exactly what it needs, so nothing is re-typed and every number on the dashboard traces back to a real record.

  1. 1Capture

    Portal, website, referral or walk-in — one form, duplicate-safe contacts.

  2. 2Assign

    Round-robin inside the team, or hand-picked. Ownership history kept.

  3. 3Match

    Listings that meet every must-have, with the reason for each match.

  4. 4View

    Book viewings on shared calendars and record what the client said.

  5. 5Negotiate

    Offer, negotiation and reservation stages with full history.

  6. 6Close

    Won deals update the listing, the leaderboard and gross commission.
